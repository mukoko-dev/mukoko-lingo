## 📞 Strategic Questions Answered

### "Why not just Zimbabwe?"
**Answer**: Zimbabwe validates the model, but the real opportunity is pan-African. 1.4B people vs 15M people. We can serve the entire continent with the same platform foundation (Noto Serif, scalable architecture, proven pedagogy).

### "Why not European/Asian languages?"
**Answer**: Duolingo et al already dominate that space. African languages are underserved, and we can build authentic community-driven content that larger platforms cannot match. Blue ocean strategy.

### "Can you really support 50+ languages?"
**Answer**: Yes, with Noto Serif as the foundation. All major African languages are supported by this single font family. Add local native speakers for content + AI for practice = scalable model.

### "How do you compete with free resources?"
**Answer**: We don't compete - we organize. Free resources are scattered and inconsistent. We provide structure, verification, AI practice, progress tracking, certificates, and community. Freemium model lets users start free, upgrade for premium features.

### "What about low internet connectivity?"
**Answer**: Mobile-first design, data-conscious, offline mode, partnership with mobile operators for zero-rated access. Same challenges that M-Pesa solved for payments.
